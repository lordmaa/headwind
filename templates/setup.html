How riders work

Step 5 of 6 — One-time ping
What this sends, exactly
📡 A single, one-time, anonymous ping to the Headwind dev server so we have a rough idea how many instances are actually running — nothing more. It happens once, ever, right now, when you click Continue below.
🔍 Exactly two values are sent: a random id generated just now on this install (not tied to you, your IP isn't stored against it, and it can't be reversed to anything personal), and the Headwind version number. That's the whole payload — no rides, no food, no weight, no location, nothing else, ever.
🚫 Opt out forever with the toggle below — no ping is ever sent, and this can't be turned back on later short of editing the database directly.
